# Industrial Applications of MATLAB: Digital Twins & Model-Based Design

This repository highlights real-world industrial applications of MATLAB and Simulink, focusing on how Digital Twin technology and Model-Based Design (MBD) streamline engineering workflows across product lifecycles.

## Overview of Key Application Areas

### 1. Product Development

Leveraging simulation and virtual modeling early in the design cycle to accelerate innovation and reduce hardware prototyping costs.

* **Facilitate Product Design:** Development of package-handling robot digital twins to optimize kinematic design, control logic, and operational throughput *(e.g., Krones)*.

* **Virtual Verification and Validation (V&V):** Simulating elevator dynamics environments to test safety systems, ride performance, and multi-body dynamics virtually before field deployment *(e.g., Schindler Elevator)*.


### 2. Operation and Maintenance

Utilizing digital twins and data-driven algorithms to monitor live equipment, optimize real-time performance, and forecast failures.

* **Operation Optimization:** Gas turbine digital twins deployed for live performance diagnostics, parameter tuning, and operational efficiency improvements *(e.g., Siemens Energy)*.

* **Predictive Maintenance:** Dynamic scheduling of maintenance intervals during optimal operating windows to prevent catastrophic failures and minimize unplanned downtime.

### 3. Digital Twin Extends Model-Based Design
Connecting physical assets to cloud and edge analytics to enable fleet-wide intelligence and adaptive servicing.

* **Large-Scale Fleet Analytics:** Processing data streams from 50+ sensors per asset across over 100,000 machines operating in the field *(e.g., Atlas Copco)*.
* **Customer-Specific Service Strategies:** Enabling service divisions to implement customized predictive maintenance regimes based on real-time operating conditions and duty cycles.

---

## Core Technologies & Toolboxes

* **MATLAB & Simulink:** Multidomain physical modeling, algorithm development, and data analysis.
* **Simscape / Robotics System Toolbox:** Virtual prototyping, kinematics, and dynamic simulation.
* **Predictive Maintenance Toolbox:** Feature extraction, remaining useful life (RUL) estimation, and condition monitoring.
* **MATLAB Production Server / Cloud Center:** Scalable deployment for real-time fleet analytics and enterprise integration.
