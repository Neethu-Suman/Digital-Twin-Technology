## Domain Breakdown

### 1. Product Design & Multi-Physics Simulation
* **Platforms:** *ANSYS Twin Builder*, *Dassault 3DEXPERIENCE*, *MATLAB & Simulink*
* **Core Focus:** Validating physical behavior, structural/fluid dynamics, control algorithm design, reduced-order modeling (ROM), and virtual verification.

### 2. Smart Infrastructure, Civil & Built Environment
* **Platforms:** *Bentley iTwin*, *NVIDIA Omniverse*
* **Core Focus:** Large-scale geographic information systems (GIS), rail networks, smart city monitoring, and photorealistic spatial simulation.

### 3. Smart Manufacturing & Industry 4.0
* **Platforms:** *Siemens Xcelerator*, *PTC ThingWorx*, *EngenX Platform*
* **Core Focus:** End-to-end plant floor integration, operational efficiency (OEE), edge data collection, and shop-floor automation.

### 4. Enterprise IoT, Supply Chain & Logistics
* **Platforms:** *Azure Digital Twins*, *AnyLogic*
* **Core Focus:** Graph-based spatial and asset tracking across global supply chains, cloud telemetry ingestion, and discrete-event system simulation.
